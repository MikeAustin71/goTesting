
And:

$$ \ln(4) \approx 1.3862943611198906 $$

So numerically:

$$ \text{actual} + \ln(4) \approx \text{expected} $$

That means your current implementation is effectively computing:

$$ \ln(x) - \ln(4) = \ln\left(\frac{x}{4}\right) $$

$$ \ln(x) $$

The culprit is the extra `- ln(4)` term in both AGM formulas.
